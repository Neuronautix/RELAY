# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

RELAY contributors, maintainers, and community participants commit to making
participation welcoming and harassment-free, regardless of background,
identity, experience, ability, appearance, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

* communicate with empathy, respect, and technical honesty;
* give and accept constructive feedback;
* distinguish evidence, assumptions, and personal preference;
* respect confidentiality and the provenance of research or review material;
* acknowledge mistakes and work to repair their impact; and
* prioritize the health of the community and the integrity of the
  specification.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private
information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
events, and other spaces where someone represents the RELAY project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, issue
warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants
when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately where appropriate, and avoid conflicts of interest.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. If no direct private
channel is available, use a
[private GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Neuronautix/RELAY/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct`. Do not include sensitive details in a
public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
