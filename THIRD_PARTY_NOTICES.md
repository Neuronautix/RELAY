# Third-party notices

RELAY currently does not vendor or redistribute third-party source code,
schemas, specification text, or example datasets.

Development dependencies are declared in `requirements-dev.txt`, and CI actions
are declared in `.github/workflows/`. Their respective licenses apply to those
tools; they are not relicensed as part of RELAY.

Contributions that copy or adapt third-party material must identify:

* the material and its source;
* its copyright holder and license;
* whether it was copied, adapted, or generated; and
* why redistribution under the repository's MIT License is permitted.

Required notices must be added here before the material is merged. References
and semantic mappings to external standards do not copy or relicense those
standards.
