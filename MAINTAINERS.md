## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor may be nominated after sustained, constructive work on RELAY.
The nomination is discussed in a public issue and approved by the existing
maintainers. The decision must consider semantic judgment, review quality,
community conduct, and availability—not only commit volume. The pull request
that adds a maintainer must also update `.github/CODEOWNERS`.

## Inactivity and departure

Maintainers should announce planned periods of unavailability when practical.
A maintainer who has been inactive for six months may be moved to an emeritus
section after a documented attempt to contact them. A maintainer may step down
at any time. Removing access for a security or conduct reason may happen
privately first, with the public record limited to what is safe to disclose.

Until a second active maintainer joins, the lead maintainer should seek
independent subject-matter review for candidate or stable normative changes.
